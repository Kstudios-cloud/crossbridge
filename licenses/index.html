Licenses and credits

CrossBridge stands on the open work of other people. This page lists what the app uses, who made it, the license it comes under, and what we changed. Updated November 23, 2026.

3D anatomy model

The muscle and bone model is "Z-Anatomy - The libre 3D atlas of anatomy" by the Z-Anatomy project, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0). Z-Anatomy is built on "BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science" (BodyParts3D), licensed under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan.

What we changed

Muscles and bones were selected, regrouped, renamed and simplified for CrossBridge, and saved as one glTF file. Nothing else was added. This change notice applies to every copy of the model in the app and on this site.

The changed model is open too

Because the sources use ShareAlike licenses, the CrossBridge version is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. You can download it here, use it, and change it, including for commercial work, if you credit the sources above, link the license, say what you changed, and share your version under the same license.

The license covers the model file only. The CrossBridge name, app code, text and design are not part of it.
