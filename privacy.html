Privacy Policy

The short version. CrossBridge has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. What you enter stays on your phone. We do not collect it and we cannot see it.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Who we are

CrossBridge is made by Jakob Kramer, operating as KStudios in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Questions about privacy: jkramerfitness@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your phone

When you use the app, it saves these things in its own storage on your phone:

  • Your training program: goals, experience level, days, exercises, sets and reps.
  • Any injuries or sore areas you pick in the program builder, so the app can add rehab exercises and mark exercises to avoid.
  • Your workout log: exercises, weights, reps, effort, pain ratings from 0 to 10, notes (including the setup note you can write for each exercise) and dates.
  • Small settings, such as pounds or kilograms, and that you have read the welcome notice.

Some of this is health information. It is saved only on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account and no sign-in. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send anything you enter to us or to anyone else. We do not receive, store, sell or share your personal information.

The internet

The app works without an internet connection. It does not make network requests of its own. Apple runs the App Store and may collect information about downloads and device backups under Apple's own privacy policy.

Notifications

On iPhone the app can ask to send you notifications. If you allow it, the app asks your phone to show one alert when a rest timer ends. Your phone creates and shows it. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn it off under Settings in the app, or in the iPhone Settings app.

Backups and sharing

Back up creates a file on your phone. You decide where it goes, such as Files, AirDrop or email. We never receive it. Anyone who gets the file can read it, so share it with care. If you use iCloud Backup for your phone, the app's data may be part of that backup. That is controlled by your Apple settings.

Your choices

  • Erase everything: open Settings, then Erase all data on this phone.
  • Delete the app, which also removes its data from your phone.
  • Keep a copy: open Settings, then Back up.

Children

CrossBridge is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Keeping your data safe

Because your data stays on your phone, protect it with your phone's passcode and keep your phone up to date. We cannot recover lost data, so keep a backup.

Changes

If we ever add accounts, ads or anything that sends data off your phone, we will update this policy and ask you to read it again before the change applies.
