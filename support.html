Support

Questions, a mistake in an exercise, or an idea? Write to jkramerfitness@gmail.com.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings, then Back up. Choose where the file goes, such as Files or AirDrop. Do this now and then, and before you change phones or delete the app.

How do I restore a backup?

Open Settings, then Restore from a file, and pick your backup file. The app shows what is inside and asks before it replaces anything.

My program or log disappeared

The app saves only on your phone. If the app was deleted or the phone was reset, restore from a backup file. We cannot recover data for you because we never receive it.

How do I erase my data?

Open Settings, then Erase all data on this phone. Deleting the app does the same.
